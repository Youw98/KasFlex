4 · Make it your plan

Ready to use this as the day plan?

Nothing is recorded as your final day plan until you choose it.

What is real in this demo?

Electricity prices and weather inputs are real historical data. The greenhouse response, crop outcome and asset dispatch are simulated. KasFlex does not control equipment.

What makes the planner collaborative?

Your selected priority, battery reserve, gas-engine preference, daily brief, and compatible remembered preferences are passed into the planner. Changes you make are re-checked before approval.

What happens if something fails?

KasFlex keeps you on this screen and shows the failed action and its reason. It never sends you to an older interface as a fallback.
