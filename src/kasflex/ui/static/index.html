CONTEXT & LIMITATIONS

Built for research

A research instrument for energy management in horticulture.

01

A simulation, with boundaries

The default scenario uses synthetic data and an unvalidated surrogate greenhouse model. It demonstrates the planning and checking workflow; its figures are not publishable research findings.

02

Keep periods and scale separate

Validation targets the 96 m² research compartment in 2019–2020. Commercial scenarios use approximately 5 ha and dates from 2022 onwards. Compare costs per square metre and state which period each result uses.

03

Forecasts are not observations

The planner receives forecast inputs. Evaluation uses the separate actual conditions. The overview price chart displays the hourly inputs supplied to the plan.

04

The person remains in the loop

Edits require re-verification before approval. The current prototype simulates first and then records human review in an append-only audit log. Approval does not gate simulation execution.
